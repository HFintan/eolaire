Privacy Notice

AN tEOLAÍRE STEM as Gaeilge

Míníonn an fógra seo conas a bhailítear, a úsáidtear agus a stóráiltear do shonraí pearsanta nuair a chláraíonn tú san Eolaíre.

This notice explains how your personal data is collected, used and stored when you sign up to the Eolaíre.

Cé a reáchtálann an tEolaíre?Who runs the Eolaíre?

Reáchtálann an Dr Fintan Hegarty an t-eolaire seo, agus is eisean rialaitheoir na sonraí.

Teagmháil: stemasgaeilge[ag]gmail[ponc]com

The Eolaíre is maintained by Dr Fintan Hegarty, who is the data controller.

Contact: stemasgaeilge[at]gmail[dot]com

Na sonraí a bhailímidWhat we collect

Na freagraí a thugann tú san fhoirm chlárúcháin: d'ainm, do sheoladh ríomhphoist, d'institiúid nó d'eagraíocht, do ról nó do chúrsa, do réimsí oibre nó spéise, giota fút féin, nasc le próifíl (más ann dó), na rudaí a mbeadh spéis agat iontu, freagraí faoi theagasc, faoi mhaoirseacht nó faoi mhodúil trí Ghaeilge, aon tuairimí eile a roinneann tú, agus do roghanna toilithe, mar aon leis an dáta agus an t-am a seoladh an fhoirm.

The answers you give in the sign-up form: your name, email address, institution or organisation, role or course, fields of work or interest, short bio, profile link (if given), the things you'd be interested in, answers about teaching, supervision or modules through Irish, any other comments you share, and your consent choices, along with the date and time the form was submitted.

Conas a úsáidimid iadHow we use it

  • Chun teagmháil a dhéanamh leat faoi imeachtaí agus deiseanna STEM trí Ghaeilge.
  • Chun daoine a aimsiú d'imeachtaí agus do thionscadail ar leith, bunaithe ar na rudaí ar chuir tú tic leo.
  • Chun cuirí páirt a ghlacadh i staidéir taighde/imeachtaí a sheoladh ar aghaidh chugat. Ní roinnfimid do shonraí gan do chead.
  • I bhfoirm anaithnid agus chomhiomlán (m.sh. líon na ndaoine, nó ráitis gan ainm) i dtuarascálacha, in iarratais ar mhaoiniú agus in abhcóideacht ar son STEM trí Ghaeilge agus oideachas tríú leibhéal trí mheán na Gaeilge.
  • Chun tú a liostú san Eolaíre poiblí, más é sin do rogha.

Ní dhíolfar do shonraí riamh, agus ní roinnfear iad le haon duine eile gan do chead.

Ní chuirfimid do shonraí pearsanta trí uirlisí intleachta shaorga riamh.

  • To contact you about STEM events and opportunities through Irish.
  • To find people for specific events and projects, based on what you ticked.
  • To forward invitations to take part in research studies and events. We never share your details without permission.
  • In anonymised, combined form (e.g. numbers of people, or unattributed quotes) in reports, funding applications and advocacy for STEM through Irish and Irish-medium third-level education.
  • To list you in the public Eolaíre, if you choose.

Your data will never be sold, or shared with anyone else without your permission.

We will never put your personal data through AI tools.

Cad atá poiblí?What is public?

Ní fhoilsítear aon rud fút mura roghnaíonn tú a bheith san Eolaíre poiblí. Má roghnaíonn tú é sin, taispeánfar d'ainm, d'institiúid nó d'eagraíocht, do ról nó do chúrsa, do réimsí, do ghiota fút féin, do nasc (más ann dó), na gníomhaíochtaí a roghnaigh tú, agus deilbhín má tá spéis agat teagasc, maoirseacht nó staidéar a dhéanamh trí Ghaeilge.

Ní thaispeánfar do sheoladh ríomhphoist ach amháin má roghnaíonn tú é sin ar leith, agus taispeánfar é i bhfoirm a fhágann níos deacra é do ríomhairí turscair é a bhailiú. Ní thaispeántar riamh na modúil, na tionscadail ná na tráchtais a luann tú, ná aon tuairimí eile a roinneann tú.

Tá an t-eolas san Eolaíre poiblí le feiceáil ag aon duine, agus ní féidir linn smacht a choinneáil ar an gcaoi a n-úsáideann daoine eile é.

Nothing about you is published unless you choose to be in the public Eolaíre. If you do, your name, institution or organisation, role or course, fields, bio, link (if given), the activities you chose, and an icon if you are interested in teaching, supervising or studying through Irish will be shown.

Your email address is shown only if you choose that separately, and it is displayed in a form that makes it harder for spam programs to collect. The modules, projects or theses you mention and any other comments you share are never shown.

Information in the public Eolaíre can be seen by anyone, and we can't control how others use it.

An bunús dlíLegal basis

Próiseálaimid do shonraí ar bhonn do thoilithe (Airteagal 6(1)(a) den Rialachán Ginearálta maidir le Cosaint Sonraí). Is féidir leat do thoiliú a tharraingt siar am ar bith trí ríomhphost a sheoladh chuig stemasgaeilge[ag]gmail[ponc]com.

We process your data on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation). You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing stemasgaeilge[at]gmail[dot]com.

Cé eile a láimhseálann na sonraí?Who else handles the data?

Stóráiltear na freagraí i nGoogle Forms agus i nGoogle Sheets, faoi théarmaí caighdeánacha Google, agus d'fhéadfaí iad a phróiseáil lasmuigh den Aontas Eorpach. Óstáiltear suíomh an Eolaíre ar GitHub Pages, agus ní bhíonn ach an t-eolas poiblí air.

Úsáideann an suíomh clónna ó Google Fonts, rud a chiallaíonn go bhfeiceann Google seoladh IP do bhrabhsálaí. Ní úsáideann an suíomh fianáin ná anailísíocht.

Responses are stored in Google Forms and Google Sheets, under Google's standard terms, and may be processed outside the European Union. The Eolaíre website is hosted on GitHub Pages and contains only the public information.

The website uses fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google sees your browser's IP address. The website does not use cookies or analytics.

Cá fhad a choinnímid na sonraí?How long the data is stored

Coinnímid do shonraí chomh fada agus atá tú cláraithe. Gach dhá bhliain nó mar sin, seolfaimid ríomhphost chugat ag fiafraí an bhfuil tú fós sásta a bheith cláraithe. Má iarrann tú orainn do shonraí a scriosadh, scriosfar iad laistigh de 30 lá.

We keep your data for as long as you remain signed up. Every two years or so, we'll email you to ask whether you're still happy to be signed up. If you ask us to delete your data, it will be deleted within 30 days.

Do cheartaYour rights

Tá sé de cheart agat rochtain a fháil ar do shonraí, iad a cheartú, iad a scriosadh, srian a chur lena n-úsáid, cóip díobh a fháil, agus agóid a dhéanamh i gcoinne a n-úsáide. Chun aon cheann díobh seo a dhéanamh, seol ríomhphost chuig stemasgaeilge[ag]gmail[ponc]com.

Má bhíonn tú míshásta leis an gcaoi a láimhseáiltear do shonraí, is féidir leat gearán a dhéanamh leis an gCoimisiún um Chosaint Sonraí: dataprotection.ie.

You have the right to access your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict its use, receive a copy of it, and object to its use. To do any of these, email stemasgaeilge[at]gmail[dot]com.

If you're unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission: dataprotection.ie.
