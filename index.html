STEM as Gaeilge ETIMirí Comórtas

Fáilte chuig an Eolaíre! Is liosta é seo de dhaoine a bhfuil spéis acu sa Ghaeilge agus i réimsí STEM nó in ábhair ghaolmhara.

Is féidir an cuardach agus na scagairí thíos a úsáid chun daoine a aimsiú de réir réimse nó spéise. Tá fáilte roimh chách clárú, is cuma cén leibhéal Gaeilge atá agat.

Ní gá duit a bheith luaite ar an liosta poiblí: coinnítear bunachar príobháideach freisin chun daoine a chur ar an eolas faoi imeachtaí agus deiseanna. Is leor an fhoirm a líonadh agus "Nílim" a roghnú sa cheist faoin Eolaíre poiblí. Más mian leat a bheith luaite san Eolaíre poiblí amach anseo, ní gá ach ríomhphost a chur chuig stemasgaeilge[ag]gmail[ponc]com agus cuirfear leis thú.

Nóta faoin IS: Úsáideadh uirlisí intleachta shaorga chun cabhrú leis an leathanach seo a thógáil (cód HTML agus foirmlí scarbhileoige), ach ní chuirfimid do shonraí pearsanta trí uirlisí intleachta shaorga riamh.

Welcome to the Eolaíre! This is a list of people with an interest in Irish and in STEM or related fields.

You can use the search and filters below to find people by field or interest. Everyone is welcome to sign up, whatever their level of Irish.

You don't have to be listed publicly: a private database is also kept to let people know about events and opportunities. Just fill in the form and choose "Nílim" in the question about the public Eolaíre. If you'd like to be listed in the public Eolaíre in future, simply email stemasgaeilge[at]gmail[dot]com and you'll be added.

A note on AI: AI tools were used to help build this page (HTML code and spreadsheet formulas), but we will never put your personal data through AI tools.
